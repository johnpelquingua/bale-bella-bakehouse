Frequently asked questions

What types of corporate dessert orders can I request?

You can inquire about office treats, team celebrations, client or partner gifts, event dessert boxes and similar company occasions.

Are corporate orders automatically accepted?

No. Bale Bella reviews quantity, date, bake capacity, packaging, delivery and customization before confirming what can be accommodated.

Can I request branded packaging?

You may request ideas such as branded stickers, ribbons or individual packaging. Availability and additional cost, if any, are confirmed case by case.

What information should I include?

Use Corporate mode in the homepage order form and include your company or organization, estimated quantity, preferred date and any customization notes.

Can corporate orders be delivered?

Delivery may be arranged depending on destination, courier availability and order logistics. The delivery fee and final fulfillment plan are confirmed separately.
