Good to know

Quick questions.

Is my order confirmed immediately?

No. Sending the request starts the conversation. Bale Bella confirms availability, schedule, final total and payment details before the order becomes final.

When do I pay with GCash?

Only after Bale Bella confirms the order. We will send the official GCash details in your WhatsApp conversation. Send your receipt there using the same order reference.

What are the lead times?

Cookies and brownies generally need at least one day, while loaves and cheesecakes may need more. Your selected cart automatically updates the earliest selectable date.

Can I order for gifts or events?

Yes. Choose Gift or Corporate in the order form. You can add a recipient message, company details and customization requests.
