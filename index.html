Good to know

Quick questions.

Is my order confirmed immediately?

Not yet. Sending the request starts the conversation. Bale Bella will confirm product availability, schedule and payment before the order becomes final.

How does delivery work?

Select Delivery and add your location. Delivery cost is quoted separately depending on the destination and available courier.

Can I order for gifts or events?

Yes. Add your request in the Notes field, including quantities, gift message or event details. Larger orders may need additional lead time.

What if the Copy button does not work?

The app automatically uses a second copy method when browser clipboard access is unavailable. If both are blocked, it shows the full order text so you can copy it manually.
