Frequently asked questions

What brownies are on the current Bale Bella menu?

The current soft-launch menu lists Manyaman Brownie and Tablea After Dark.

What is the difference between the two brownies?

Manyaman Brownie is a classic dense dark-chocolate brownie, while Tablea After Dark adds deeper Philippine tablea character and sea salt.

Can I buy a single brownie?

Yes. Both current brownie flavors are listed as singles and as boxes of six.

How much lead time do brownies need?

The current menu lists a minimum lead time of one day for both brownie flavors.

Can brownie boxes be ordered as gifts?

Yes. Use Gift mode on the homepage order form to add the recipient name and an optional message, then wait for confirmation before payment.
