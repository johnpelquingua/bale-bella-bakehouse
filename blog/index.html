Frequently asked questions

What is the Bale Bella Journal?

The Bale Bella Journal is the bakery’s library of practical guides about fresh-bake care, dessert styles, gifting and the products on the Bale Bella menu.

Are blog posts a substitute for product-specific instructions?

No. General guides are educational. For handling or fulfillment questions about a specific Bale Bella order, use the instructions confirmed in your WhatsApp conversation.

How do I order something mentioned in an article?

Use the product and menu links inside the article or open the live homepage menu, then send the prepared order request through WhatsApp.
