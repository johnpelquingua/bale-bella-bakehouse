Why the look is different

Caramelised outside. Creamy inside.

Basque cheesecake is intentionally rustic: no need to hide the deep top colour or chase a perfectly pale surface. That contrast between browned exterior and creamy centre is the point.

Frequently asked questions

What size is Kaluguran Basque?

Kaluguran Basque is currently listed as a 15 cm whole Basque-style cheesecake.

How much is Kaluguran Basque?

How much lead time does the cheesecake need?

Why is Basque cheesecake dark on top?

The deeply browned, caramelised top is a defining feature of the Basque style rather than a decoration added after baking.
