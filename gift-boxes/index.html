Frequently asked questions

What can I order as a Bale Bella gift?

Current giftable options include cookie boxes, brownie boxes, the Bale Bella Taste Box, Bella’s Banana Loaf and Kaluguran Basque cheesecake.

How do I mark an order as a gift?

Choose Gift in the homepage order form. That reveals fields for the recipient name and an optional short gift message.

Can a gift be delivered directly to the recipient?

Delivery may be arranged in and around San Fernando subject to destination, courier availability and a separately quoted delivery fee.

When should I pay for a gift order?

Pay only after Bale Bella confirms the products, date, fulfillment details and final amount through WhatsApp.

Do you handle larger office or client gifting?

Yes. Use the Corporate order option for teams, client lists, events or larger quantities so feasibility and customization can be reviewed first.
