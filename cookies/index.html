Frequently asked questions

What cookie flavors does Bale Bella currently offer?

The current soft-launch menu lists Mayumu Chunk, Tablea Trouble, Ube Keso Please and Biscoff Ka Pa.

Can I order cookies as singles or boxes?

Yes. Each current cookie flavor has a single price plus box-of-four and box-of-six variants shown on the live menu.

How much lead time do Bale Bella cookies need?

The current menu lists a minimum lead time of one day for cookies. The live order form may require a later date depending on the rest of your cart.

Where can I pick up a cookie order?

Confirmed pre-order pickup is from Bale Bella Bakehouse in Madonna Residences, Barangay Dela Paz Sur, City of San Fernando, Pampanga.

Can cookie boxes be delivered?

Delivery may be arranged in and around San Fernando subject to courier availability and a separately quoted delivery fee.
