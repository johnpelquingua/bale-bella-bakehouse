Frequently asked questions

Is this the same menu used for ordering?

This page is the permanent Bale Bella menu guide. For live availability, box variants, preferred date and checkout, use the interactive menu and order form on the homepage.

How far ahead should I order?

Cookies and brownies currently list at least one day of lead time. Banana loaf, Basque cheesecake and the Bale Bella Taste Box currently list at least two days. The live order form calculates the earliest selectable date from your cart.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

Yes. Pre-order pickup is available from Dela Paz Sur, City of San Fernando. Delivery may be arranged in and around San Fernando subject to courier availability and a separately quoted fee.

When should I pay by GCash?

Pay only after Bale Bella confirms availability, schedule, fulfillment and the final amount through the same WhatsApp conversation.

Can I order for gifts or corporate events?

Yes. Choose Gift or Corporate in the homepage order form so the relevant recipient, company, quantity and customization fields appear.
